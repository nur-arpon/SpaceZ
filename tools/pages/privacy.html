Privacy

Right now, nothing.

This page is about this website, nur-arpon.github.io/SpaceZ. Each SpaceZ app has its own privacy policy, linked from its own page. Last updated 13 October 2026.

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no cookies and no sign-in. The fonts, pictures and scripts are served from this website itself.

The project form

The "Start a project" form runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to this website or stored anywhere. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", it opens WhatsApp with the message written out, and nothing is sent until you press Send there. From then on, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.

Who hosts it

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical details every browser sends when it loads a page, such as your IP address. SpaceZ does not see or keep those. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles them.

Things your browser remembers

If you switch between the Black and Navy looks, your browser remembers that choice on your own device (in local storage). It never leaves your device.

What may change

If visitor counting is ever added, this page will say so first: which service is used, what it counts and how to opt out.
