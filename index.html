How it works

Five steps, no surprises. You always know what is happening, what comes next and what it will cost.

Step 1Brief

Tell us what you need, in your own words. The form below makes it quick.

Step 2Plan

We reply with a plan and a quote: what gets built, in what order, and by when.

Step 3Build

Small steps you can see. You get real builds to try, not just progress reports.

Step 4Launch

Website, store listing, privacy policy and search setup, shipped properly.

Step 5Support

Launch is not goodbye. Fixes and updates after launch are part of the plan.

Questions

Asked before you ask. Something else on your mind? Put it in your message.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so there is no price list. Tell us about yours and we will reply with a plan and a quote before any work starts.

Who will actually build it?

SpaceZ was founded by Nur Ifran Arpon, an engineering student and developer. He builds with a small team of student developers, the same people who made Spaceadom, Spike and FileZ, and he is the person you talk to.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere. Most of the work happens in writing, and calls are set for a time that suits you.

Can you put my app on the Microsoft Store?

Yes. SpaceZ has published three apps there: Spaceadom, Spike and FileZ. That covers the listing, the screenshots, the privacy policy and the submission itself.

Can my website be in Bangla and English?

Yes. Hashi Khushi and Mistri Lagbe both switch between Bangla and English on every page, and we can do the same for any two languages.

What if I only have a rough idea?

That is the normal place to start. Describe the problem you want solved, and the plan will turn it into something concrete.
